About Held & Bound

I am
Kaylee-Jane.

I grew up around strong spiritual women, ritual, symbolism and belief. As an adult, I followed my own path — personal, eclectic, and rooted in the idea of one Source, whatever name you give it.

Held & Bound took form in 2026, but the instinct behind it began much earlier.

After art school and years of working across different mediums, I found myself back at my studio table wanting to make something tactile, beautiful and purposeful. Something to hold, wear or keep close.

So I returned to clay.

Held & Bound is made slowly, by hand, in small batches, using clay, botanicals, texture and symbolic marks. I keep the irregularities and traces of the making process because they are part of the object.

For me, the process is part craft, part ritual, part meditation.
Every object begins with a purpose and a clear intention.

Something considered.
Something made by hand.
Something to hold, wear, keep close — and make your own.

01 — Material

Three materials only. Raw Clay + Tobacco Clay + Botanical Hard Bake.

Raw Clay is pale, natural and minimal. Tobacco Clay is deep brown with a worn, aged leather look. Botanical Hard Bake is a lighter hand-formed blend with lavender, basil and coarse black pepper, giving each piece a soft cream tone and visible organic texture.

02 — Mark

Symbols, bind runes & custom marks — chosen for meaning.

Algiz ᛉ for protection, Fehu ᚠ and Jera ᛃ for prosperity, growth and harvest, and Algiz + Othala ᛉᛟ for home, boundaries and sanctuary. These are some of the marks I return to most, but each piece can carry a different rune, bind rune, botanical motif, personal mark, or something completely unique. Every mark is carved by hand.

03 — Ritual

Made slowly, finished with blessing.

Every piece is formed by hand in small batches, dried slowly, fired, and finished with a simple blessing ritual before leaving the studio.
