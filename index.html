Made with intention

Every Held & Bound piece is made slowly, by hand, with a specific intention in mind.

The herbs, symbol and form are chosen deliberately, and each piece is finished with a simple blessing ritual before it leaves the studio.

For customers who want something more personal, you can choose Personal Attunement at checkout.

You may send:

  • a photo
  • a name
  • a date of birth
  • or simply a written intention

Your piece will then be made and finished with that person or intention specifically in mind.

Photos are optional and deleted after the attunement is complete.
