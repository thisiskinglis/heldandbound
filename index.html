Botanical Materials

Bound with real herbs — flour, salt, oil, PVA wood glue, water

Your recipe with new wood glue: 1 cup flour, 1/2 cup fine salt, 1 tbsp oil, 1 tbsp PVA wood glue, 1/3-1/2 cup warm water. Knead 5-7 mins, add rosemary/sage/lavender/basil/pepper. Air dry 24h, then 100C 2.5-3h. Non-edible, craft use only. For UK/US customs, list ingredients as "flour, salt, dried herbs, PVA glue".
